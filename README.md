An original 3D open-world crime game built for the [make.fun GTA 6 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01).
Mara Solano is back in **Solmar City** — a sun-soaked procedural city with a beach, a harbor, parks and a
downtown of towers — with nothing but a borrowed jacket and a score to settle.

## What you can do

- **Explore on foot** — third-person WASD + mouse-look, jump, sprint, through streets, the plaza, the beach and the docks.
- **Drive anything** — eight vehicle types (sedan, taxi, sports car, van, police cruiser, city bus, armored van, limousine) plus the **Solmar Comet 900 motorcycle** and the **Boulevard Cruiser**, which lean into turns and outrun everything on the road. Press **E** to steal a car or bike, pull the driver out, and go.
- **Live city** — traffic AI that follows the roads and stops for cars ahead, buses on the avenues, pedestrians who stroll the sidewalks and flee from gunfire — joggers, tourists, elders with canes, friends walking in pairs.
- **Wanted 0–5 stars** — crimes raise the heat; Solmar PD cruisers chase you, roadblocks appear at 3+ stars, officers on foot will arrest you if they get close — and at 4+ stars a **police helicopter** hunts you from the sky with a searchlight that stops the heat from cooling off. Break line of sight to cool off.
- **Combat** — fists (**F**) and a pistol (mouse click, **Q** to switch, **R** to reload).
- **Nine story missions** — "First Wheels", "The Pickup", "Harbor Run", "Heat Rising", "Solmar Nights" and "The Long Runway" at the **Solmar Airfield** (runway, terminal, control tower, hangars, parked planes), then the **Verde Heist** and the v4 heist arc ("The Solmar Job", "King of Solmar") — with dialogue, waypoints, timed runs and cash rewards — plus a repeatable taxi job (**J**), a courier run (**C**, three timed deliveries), stunt-jump ramps that pay for big air, and twelve hidden cash packages. Mission 10, "The Last Sunset", closes the story in v5.
- **v3 — street racing** — press **G** at the chequered start banner by the plaza to race three circuits (**Downtown Loop**, **Harbor Sprint**, **Airfield GP**) against three AI rivals with rubber-banding, live positions and cash prizes, on a season ladder that saves.
- **v3 — Vic's garage** — bring your car to the garage and spend cash on a respray (**U**, eight paints), engine upgrades (**I**, +8% top speed, three levels) and armor plating (**O**). Your tuned car persists in the save. Engine Lv1+ unlocks **nitro** (hold **Shift** for +35% top speed, with a meter and a blue exhaust flame), and **Y** buys neon underglow that glows at night.
- **v3 — Mission 7, "The Verde Heist"** — the story finale: lift the casino's take from the Isla Verde marina, run it to the airfield under a city-wide alarm, lose the heat and bring the money home to the plaza — then the epilogue screen closes Mara's arc.
- **v3 — Isla Verde** — a second district across the causeway bridge: boardwalk shops, a lighthouse that sweeps the bay at night, a marina, a resort hotel, palm rows and its own tourists. Courier runs sometimes end there.
- **v3 — boats** — six stealable, drivable boats in the harbor and the Isla Verde marina (**E** to board), including the fast **Verde Comet** speedboat. Heavy boats, a bobbing hull, a foam wake, and rain that chops up the swell. The PD waits on the shore — cruisers cannot follow you onto the water. Sail the five orange harbor-tour buoys in order for $500, or the five gold marina-tour buoys in the bay south of Isla Verde for $750 — both repeatable.
- **v3 — racing ladder** — four circuits now (Downtown, Harbor, Airfield, Isla Verde GP), each keeping its own fastest time, and the pause menu shows Mara's full **career stats**: story progress, race wins, bounties, tours, hidden packages, garage tuning and time in Solmar.
- **v3 — safehouse** — walk into Mara's yellow house to clear the wanted level, heal up, restock pistol ammo, refill the nitro tank and change outfit (the jacket cycles through five colours).
- **v3 — radio** — press **N** to cycle five synthesized stations: **Solmar FM** (synthwave), **Radio Verde** (latin groove), **Talk Solmar** (late-night callers), **Harbor Rock** and **Solmar After Dark** (v5) — each with its own DJ greeting you on tune-in, and Solmar News bulletins that cut in with the city's latest.
- **v3 — vigilante contracts** — press **V** while driving to hunt a fugitive in a stolen Comet GT: ram it three times or pin it for two seconds and the PD pays a $600 bounty. At 3+ stars the PD fights back with **spike strips** and extra **roadblocks**. Race winners also keep a fastest-time record per circuit, saved with the ladder.
- **v5 — photo mode** — press **B** anywhere in free roam and the world freezes for the camera: drag the mouse to orbit, **Q** cycles five film filters (Solmar Gold, Midnight Blue, Noir, Verde Pop, Dawn Haze), **R** hides the HUD, **Space** snaps a PNG to your downloads. The Sunset Viewpoint deck on the beach and the lighthouse overlook on Isla Verde are built for it.
- **v5 — the casino table floor** — inside the Verde Casino, the **TABLE GAMES** button opens **roulette** ($10/$25/$50/$100 chips on red, black, green 0 or a lucky number — colour pays 2x, exact number 36x) and **blackjack** ($50 a hand, dealer stands on 17, natural 21 pays 3:2). Every chip flows through the night's tally.
- **v5 — Radio v2** — two new stations join the dial: **Harbor Rock 104.7** (guitars for the docks) and **Solmar After Dark 95.6** (slow funk for the drive home), and **Solmar News bulletins** now cut in every minute and a half — traffic, weather and crime flashes that react to the wanted level and the story.
- **v5 — Mission 10, "The Last Sunset"** — the story coda after "King of Solmar": golden hour at the Isla Verde lighthouse, a 90-second sprint to the airfield, the sunset crate from hangar A, the whole PD surging in, and a toast at the beach bar. $9,000 and the epilogue now closes ten missions.
- **v5 — night racing** — two new circuits join the rotation: the **Uptown Loop** and the **Midnight Beach Run**, which borrows the midnight sky and pays a 1.5x purse. Night wins are kept on the ladder.
- **v5 — new rides to steal** — the **Solmar Armored Van** (plated, shrugs off walls, parked by the museum bank block) and the **Verde Limousine** (long, low, gold-trimmed, parked outside the casino), plus a **Boulevard Cruiser** motorcycle at the beach bar and the fast **Verde Comet** speedboat in the marina. Armored vans and limousines also join the traffic mix.
- **v5 — safehouse v2** — press **L** near the house to open **Mara's Wardrobe**: four outfits with real perks (Harbor Runner sprints 15% faster, Night Stalker cools heat 50% faster, Iron Vest adds 50 armor, Boardwalk Star heals 25), plus a **weapon locker** selling ammo boxes, armor vests and medkits.
- **v5 — garage v2** — **H** buys sport suspension (+12% steering response, two levels) and **K** fits a bigger nitro tank (+25 to the bottle, filled on the spot).
- **v5 — street life** — a guitarist on the plaza and a drummer at the beach bar play for a ring of onlookers (tip them $5 and the crowd cheers), three vendor stalls sell fruit, flowers and boardwalk snacks, and tourists now raise their cameras and flash at the plaza fountain, the lighthouse and the beach bar.
- **v6 — Old Town** — a historic quarter in the north-east: the **Cathedral of Saint Mara**, a cobbled plaza with a fountain, a market street of six stalls, a **tram** gliding the old avenue, narrow townhouses and a bell tower. Landmarks pay $50 a visit, and the quarter has its own crowd — market shoppers and tourists with cameras.
- **v6 — Missions 11–12** — **"Old Town Rising"** takes the quarter back from the Harbor Syndicate (hold the cathedral steps, race the market shakedown, lift the ledger at the tram station), and **"The Mountain Run"** crosses the causeway at dawn for a 75-second climb to the ski lodge. $5,500 and $7,000 — the story now closes twelve missions.
- **v6 — VIP jobs & parcels** — press **X** for a **VIP pickup** (Senator Vela, Madame Okafor and six more high-rollers who tip for fast, smooth, star-free driving) or a **parcel run** against the clock. Both feed a driver rating that raises the fares.
- **v6 — Verde poker** — press **Z** to sit at the **high-roller Texas Hold'em table**: $100 buys 1,000 chips, three AI regulars (cool Lena, bluffing Vic, careful Rosa) play a full flop, turn and river, and chips cash out at 10:1.
- **v6 — the National Guard** — at **five stars** the PD steps aside: an **armored personnel carrier** and a squad of soldiers take over the chase, with heavier fire and no interest in arrests. Drop below three stars and they stand down.
- **v6 — car collection** — every distinct car Mara drives joins her **collection** (up to five). Call any collected car to her location from the **pause menu**, and watch them appear on the **showroom plinths** outside Vic's garage.
- **v6 — the mountain** — a snow-capped peak on Isla Verde with a winding trail, a **ski lodge** with a glowing hearth, a summit viewpoint over the bay, snow pines and a frozen pond. Walk into the snow zone and the weather turns to **snowfall** on its own; the first viewpoint visit pays $200.
- **v6 — more ways to move** — a **charter flight** from the airfield apron (E to board — a free scenic loop around the bay), the **Old Town ferry** running the harbor loop to the marina, a drivable **city bus** that earns $5-a-head fares along its eight-stop route, and a **fire engine** with a working light bar and two-tone siren that clears traffic. A **Trail Blazer 250** dirt bike waits at the mountain trailhead, and **nitro refill stations** top the tank up free.
- **v6 — combat & stamina** — **Q** now cycles fists → pistol → **SMG** (fast spray, lighter rounds, wider spread), and sprinting runs on a **stamina meter** that drains on the run and refills on the walk.
- **v6 — Radio v3** — **Solmar Sports & Traffic 98.2** joins the dial with play-by-play calls and traffic reports, and the talk show's **Host Dana follows Mara's rap sheet live** — the hotter the chase, the hotter the segment. A **news chopper** circles at 3+ stars to film it all.
- **v6 — racing** — two new circuits: the **Old Town GP** through the quarter's narrow streets and the **Summit Run** across the causeway to the ski lodge.
- **v6 — photo mode v2** — while the camera is out, **N** cycles the time of day (dawn, noon, golden hour, blue hour, midnight) and **T** cycles the weather (including snow), and a **landmark checklist** tracks which of Solmar's sights Mara has framed.
- **v6 — second safehouse & more** — a stone cottage in Old Town clears the heat just like the first house, **armored-takedown** vigilante contracts pay double, **stunt chains** pay 25% more per linked jump, and the pause-menu scorecard tracks VIP jobs, poker, the collection, Guard deployments and landmarks.
- **v5 — the living city, deeper** — a **golden haze** weather mode (T cycles it in) that pulls the fog in low over the bay, **rush-hour traffic** that pushes 15% harder from 17:00–19:00, a **5-star searchlight sweep** from the air unit that only freezes the heat while it passes over Mara, **stakeout** vigilante contracts (tail the suspect 20 seconds, 12–55m back, don't get spotted), and a career scorecard that now tracks night wins, blackjack hands, outfits, snapshots and tips.
- **v4 — Missions 8–9, the heist arc** — after the Verde Heist, "The Solmar Job" cracks the National Bank's vault on a 75-second silent-alarm timer (the drill sparks and rattles while Mara holds the crack), and "King of Solmar" runs a gold getaway car from the airfield to the harbor for the last shipment — $8,000 and $12,000, and the epilogue now closes nine missions.
- **v4 — flyable helicopters** — a **PD Hawk** on the airfield helipad (stealing it is a crime — two stars) and a **News Chopper** at the marina. **E** to climb in; W/S pitches the nose, A/D yaws, **Space** climbs, **Shift** sinks. Rotor wash kicks dust up when you hover low; land on a rooftop, a runway or the beach and press **E** to hop out.
- **v4 — the Street League** — two new circuits join the rotation (**Beachfront Sprint** and the three-lap **Solmar Grand Prix** full-city loop), and every race now scores championship points against Rico, Tasha and Benny. Reach 30 points to unlock the Grand Prix — win it and Mara is crowned **Solmar Champion** with a $5,000 purse.
- **v4 — jet skis** — three of them bobbing at the Isla Verde marina (**E** to ride). Carve the bay at 115 km/h and run the six-buoy **marina time trial** in order for $600, with your best time kept on the board.
- **v4 — the Verde Casino** — walk up to the gold doors on the boardwalk and press **E**. The **slots** take $25 a spin (three 7s pays the $500 jackpot) and the **high/low** table takes a $50 bet and pays $100 when you call the next card right. Winnings and losses are real cash, and the house keeps a tally of the night.
- **v4 — street events** — every minute or two the city throws something at Mara: a **bank getaway** car tearing across town dropping cash (ram it to stop it, +$400), a **block takeover** to hold on foot for 20 seconds (+$500), or a **most-wanted** fugitive in a tan van to catch (+$600).
- **v4 — fog and thunderstorms** — storm fronts roll in off the bay: lightning forks down near Mara, thunder rolls a beat later, the fog closes to a few dozen metres and the rain hammers until the front passes. Fog banks can also roll in on their own on a clear morning and burn off by midday.
- **A living sky** — a full **day/night cycle**: the sun arcs across the sky, dusk turns the city orange, and at night the streetlights come on, windows glow, the moon rises, and driving needs real headlights. **Weather** (**T**) brings light and heavy rain: wet roads darken and grip falls, so cars slide and bikes get twitchy.
- **HUD + minimap** — health/armor, cash, wanted stars, clock, mission tracker, speedometer and a live canvas minimap.
- **Sound** — fully synthesized WebAudio: engine hum, sirens, gunshots, footsteps and a synthwave city loop. No audio files.
- **Save** — progress (cash, missions, packages, racing ladder, league season, garage tuning, radio station, bounties, jet-ski best time) saves automatically to localStorage.

## Play it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Everything is static files under `game/` (three.js vendored as one minified module). No network requests at runtime, no build step.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| WASD / mouse | Move / look |
| Space / Shift | Jump / sprint — **Space** climbs / **Shift** sinks in a helicopter |
| E | Enter or exit a car, boat, helicopter, jet ski — or the casino |
| B | Photo mode (Q filter · R hide HUD · Space snapshot) |
| L | Safehouse wardrobe & weapon locker (near the safehouse) |
| H / K | Garage suspension / nitro tank (at Vic's garage) |
| F | Punch |
| Click / Q / R | Fire pistol / switch weapon (Q again for SMG) / reload |
| X | VIP / parcel job (rating pays) |
| Z | Verde poker table ($100 buy-in) |
| J | Start a taxi job |
| C | Start a courier run (3 timed deliveries) |
| T | Cycle the weather (clear / light rain / heavy rain) |
| P / Esc | Pause |
| M | Mute |

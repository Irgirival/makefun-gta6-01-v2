MARA SOLANO · AN OPEN-WORLD STORY

Mara Solano came back to Solmar City with nothing but a borrowed jacket and a score to settle. Steal cars, outrun the Solmar PD, run jobs for the people who run this town — and take the city back, one mission at a time. New in this version: photo mode with film filters, roulette and blackjack at the Verde Casino, two more radio stations with live news bulletins, Mission 10 "The Last Sunset", night racing, an armored van and a limousine to steal, an outfit shop with perks, street performers and vendors, and a golden-hour haze over the bay. New in v6: the Old Town quarter with its cathedral, market and tram; Missions 11–12; VIP jobs and parcel runs; Texas Hold'em poker at the Verde; the National Guard at five stars; a five-car collection with a garage showroom; a snow-capped mountain with a ski lodge; a charter flight, a harbor ferry, a drivable fire engine and city bus; and a fourth radio station that follows your rap sheet.
